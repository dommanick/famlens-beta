FamLens Beta Terms

FamLens is provided as a closed beta shopping assistant. By using it, you understand that results may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be checked before making important decisions.

Use responsibly

Use FamLens for product labels, receipts, shopping help, and family purchase records. Do not upload sensitive documents, payment cards, government IDs, or unrelated private information.

No medical advice

FamLens may explain nutrition labels, ingredients, product warnings, and usage instructions, but it does not provide medical advice. Consult professionals for health, medication, allergy, supplement, eye care, skin care, or emergency questions.

Beta availability

The beta may change, fail, or become unavailable while we improve the product. Features, languages, and reports may be adjusted during testing.

Feedback

Feedback you submit may be used to improve FamLens and guide future product decisions.
