FamLens Privacy Notice

FamLens helps immigrant families understand products, receipts, and household shopping records.

What we collect

When you use FamLens, we may process product photos, receipt photos, selected language, optional family notes, AI chat questions, anonymous device ID, and usage events such as scans, saves, shares, and feedback.

How we use it

We use this information to analyze products and receipts, generate summaries, improve accuracy, understand product usage, and operate the service.

Health and purchase information

FamLens gives practical shopping information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical conditions, allergies, medications, supplements, or eye/skin products, ask a doctor, pharmacist, or qualified professional.

Product data

Uploaded shopping images and usage logs may be reviewed to improve the service. Do not upload private documents unrelated to shopping.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the person who shared FamLens with you.
