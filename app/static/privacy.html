FamLens Privacy Notice

FamLens is currently a closed beta product for immigrant families and household shoppers.

What we collect

When you use FamLens, we may process product photos, receipt photos, selected language, optional family notes, AI chat questions, anonymous device ID, and usage events such as scans, saves, shares, and feedback.

How we use it

We use this information to analyze products and receipts, generate summaries, improve accuracy, understand beta usage, and operate the product.

Health and purchase information

FamLens gives practical shopping information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical conditions, allergies, medications, supplements, or eye/skin products, ask a doctor, pharmacist, or qualified professional.

Beta data

During beta, uploaded images and usage logs may be reviewed by the product team to improve the service. Do not upload private documents unrelated to shopping.

Contact

If you are testing FamLens through an invitation, contact the person who invited you with privacy or deletion requests.
