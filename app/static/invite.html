For the person who shops for the family

Scan once

Open it with the phone camera, browser, or a shared QR code.

2

Add to home screen

On iPhone, use Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the browser menu then Install App.

3

Use it in the store

After the FamLens icon appears, it opens like a regular app.

Use notes

  • For products, capture the front, back, or instruction label clearly.
  • For receipts, include the full item list and total amount.
  • Food, supplement, personal care, and household results are for shopping reference.

For illness, medication, allergy, eye, or skin concerns, ask a doctor, pharmacist, or qualified professional.
